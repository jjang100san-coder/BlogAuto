THE THREE FOREIGN-LANGUAGE QUOTES ARE THE BACKBONE
--------------------------------------------------
Korean and English searches turned up nothing on how Moreno picks a squad.
Searching in the languages of the countries he worked in did:
  FR (Monaco) - "I do not think the same eleven should start every time...
                 I want two players in every position, experience mixed
                 with young players."
  RU (Sochi)  - "We always pick the starting eleven based on what condition
                 the players are in." A 19-year-old started that match.
  ES          - "The system is not important. I apply the system to the
                 players, never the players to the system."
This is what makes the post different from every other preview. If anything
here is cut, cut something else first.

THIS IS A FRIENDLY IN A FOUR-MATCH RUN
--------------------------------------
Moreno said he brought in players who had never been called up and wants
them all to have their moment, and that accumulated load may force changes.
The body argues a near-identical XI would waste the test. Card 09 and the
card 07 labels both reflect that. Do not turn 07 back into a fixed XI.

LEE DONG-GYEONG - HIS ROLE CAME FROM THE MANAGER
------------------------------------------------
He said at the press conference that Moreno told him in their first meeting
he would play as a second striker - the same role he plays at his club.
That role overlaps with where Lee Kang-in would go. Three players (Lee
Kang-in, Lee Dong-gyeong, Kim Min-su) sit in that one slot on card 09.

PAIK SEUNG-HO - CHECKED, 2026-09-27
-----------------------------------
He is a holding midfielder at his club, not an attacking one. Birmingham did
push him one line higher at the start of this season (Sheffield Utd, Bristol
City) - both ended in draws, local media said the midfield needed players
with more running and bite, he was dropped, and the team won next time out.
So under Moreno's "play them where their club plays them" rule he belongs in
the SITTING slot, not the advanced one. The body says this without naming him.
Do not move him to the advanced slot.

Moreno said at the press conference he wants the new call-ups to get their
moment. That quote is in the body and is the reason the slot is left open.
