NOTES
  All filenames are ASCII on purpose. The [[IMG:...]] markers inside the draft
  match these names exactly - do not rename the files.
  Upload images with "choose file" or drag and drop. Pasting a screenshot with
  Ctrl+V renames the file and breaks the markers.
  01-05 were drawn from scratch. No broadcast frames, stat-site screenshots,
  newspaper rating tables, channel logos or faces are used anywhere.
